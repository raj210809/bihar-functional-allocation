Method note — the counterfactual problem

Outcomes below cannot be causally attributed to any single list entry without a counterfactual. Where possible this dashboard leans on inter-state variation (professional tax, land reform) and before/after series (NFHS-5 → NFHS-6). At least one outcome variable per function comes from an independent survey or audit rather than the implementing department's own portal — these carry a green Survey or navy Audit stamp. Indirect critical factors (political economy, jurisdictional overlap, social structure) appear in the amber box under each function: they are named, not claimed as measured.
